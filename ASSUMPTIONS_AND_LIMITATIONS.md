# Assumptions, evidence, and limitations register

This register separates supplied facts, measured evidence, design choices,
assumptions, and unresolved unknowns. It is part of the submitted reasoning,
not a list of facts that the implementation silently treats as true.

The concise method story is in [`README.md`](README.md) and the report. This file is the
authoritative risk register: the main workflow mentions only assumptions that
change the method, evaluation validity, or deliverable semantics.

## Production evaluation that is still missing

A safety claim would require substantially more than these two traversals:
route-level and geography-level held-out data, multiple dates and seasons,
independent annotators with disagreement adjudication, explicit changed-scene and
no-overlap cases, calibration curves with confidence intervals, and monitoring
of false accepts at the chosen operating coverage. None of those are inferred
from this take-home dataset.
