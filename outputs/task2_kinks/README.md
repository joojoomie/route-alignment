# Mapping kinks: implausible one-step jumps

Generated by `scripts/analyze_mapping_kinks.py`. Label-free except for the
cross-check in section 3, which reads the sealed blind labels but tunes nothing
against them.

## What a kink is

A mapping's first difference is a local speed ratio. Consecutive Run A frames
are 0.1 s apart, so mapping them to Run B frames `b` and `b + d` asserts that
Run B needed `d` frames to cover what Run A covered in one - a `d`-fold speed
ratio at that instant. `d = 1` is equal speed. The pipeline's own coarse search
is bounded to a sustained slope in [0.2, 2.0] with a largest
single step of 3.0x, so **`d >= 4` is already outside what the model that
produced the path may believe, and `d >= 6` (the headline threshold `K`) is not
physically reachable in 0.1 s.** A stop does not produce a kink; a stop makes
Run B repeat frames (`d = 0`).

A kink is not proof of an error. It is a label-free statement that the mapping
asserts something its own motion model forbids.

Pairs straddling an abstention gap are excluded: a jump across a hole in Run A
is the hole, not a speed claim.

## 2. Cross-camera corroboration

Disagreement is measured as `evaluate_task2_consistency.py` does: `cam0 - cam5`
minus a 201-frame running median, because the two cameras are not
proved synchronised and only the *variation* of their difference is evidence.
For the submitted mapping, cam0: 20 of 27 kinks sit within 10 Run A frames of a >6-frame cross-camera disagreement; cam5: 18 of 25 kinks sit within 10 Run A frames of a >6-frame cross-camera disagreement.

## 5. Mechanism

Reproduced on cam0 over Run A 880-940 from the cached stage-1 descriptors.

* The **coarse DTW path is smooth** there: its largest one-step Run B increment is **3**, and it contains 0 increments >= K.
* The **submitted mapping is not**: largest one-step increment **11**, with 2 increments >= K in the same window.
* Route-wide, the refined-minus-coarse residual is bounded to [-8, 8] and sits pinned at the +8 window edge on 74 frames.
* Replaying stage 3 with the real structure descriptors reproduces the submitted values exactly (61/61 frames). The initial jump is the unconstrained argmax of a *collapsed* structure score, not a constraint artefact - but the non-decreasing constraint then changed the pick on 27 of 61 frames, at times leaving as few as 1 admissible candidate(s) out of 17.
* On 27 of those frames the free argmax sits a median 9.0 Run B frames *behind* the submitted value, and scores better there (0.6501 vs 0.4242). The descriptors say "go back"; the non-decreasing constraint forbids it.
* Root cause: stage 3 runs at full cadence with a monotonicity constraint but **no slope prior**. The coarse stage has one ([0.2, 2.0]) and does not kink here; the Bayes posterior, which carries an explicit transition model, produces 1 and 1 (cam0, cam5) against the submitted mapping's 27 and 25.
* `variant:grey` over the same window and the same descriptors: largest one-step increment **13**, 1 increments >= K, 46 frames pinned at the window edge, median structure score **0.4314** against the submitted mapping's 0.5212 (free argmax 0.6513); it scores at least as well as the submitted pick on 31 of 61 frames and equals the free argmax on 3. Label-free.
* `variant:parallax` over the same window and the same descriptors: largest one-step increment **10**, 2 increments >= K, 0 frames pinned at the window edge, median structure score **0.4661** against the submitted mapping's 0.5212 (free argmax 0.6513); it scores at least as well as the submitted pick on 32 of 61 frames and equals the free argmax on 22. Label-free.
* `variant:sky` over the same window and the same descriptors: largest one-step increment **11**, 2 increments >= K, 19 frames pinned at the window edge, median structure score **0.5212** against the submitted mapping's 0.5212 (free argmax 0.6513); it scores at least as well as the submitted pick on 61 of 61 frames and equals the free argmax on 34. Label-free.
* `variant:sky+grey` over the same window and the same descriptors: largest one-step increment **13**, 1 increments >= K, 46 frames pinned at the window edge, median structure score **0.4314** against the submitted mapping's 0.5212 (free argmax 0.6513); it scores at least as well as the submitted pick on 31 of 61 frames and equals the free argmax on 3. * `variant:slope` over the same window and the same descriptors: largest one-step increment **3**, 0 increments >= K, 0 frames pinned at the window edge, median structure score **0.6018** against the submitted mapping's 0.5212 (free argmax 0.6513); it scores at least as well as the submitted pick on 50 of 61 frames and equals the free argmax on 36. Label-free.

**Conclusion: the jump is introduced by the stage-3 refinement, not by the coarse DTW path, and is made persistent by the non-decreasing constraint.**
